During the call

Needs your microphone transcribed, under Listening. When the other side speaks again after one of your answers, the saved model says whether it was on point, missed the point, ran long or drifted, with a short note. One extra request per answer, at most 20 a call, sent only while no answer is being written. Applies from the next call.

For a call where money comes up. The lines cover anchors, counters and what to trade when the number cannot move, and they leave the deciding to you. Figures come only from your documents or from what was said on the call: nothing is a going rate the model made up, and nothing has you claim an offer or a competing price you do not have. Turn it off for ordinary calls. Applies from the next call.

After the call

Off unless you turn it on. Each call becomes one file: what the other side said, what you said when your microphone is transcribed, the questions and the lines shown. Never the audio. The summary at the end of a call does not need it. While it is on, the call's diagnostic logs (including the raw speech-to-text output) are kept in %APPDATA%\Fawa2ly\sessions too; while it is off, nothing about a call is written.

                      Listening

                      Fawa2ly hears the call through the speakers or headset it plays on. These apply from the next call.

                      For a call in Arabic choose Arabic: Auto does not include it, and the lines come back in the language chosen here.

                      Transcribes on this computer, so no audio leaves it and no Deepgram key is needed. A question is judged only once the other side stops speaking, so the lines come later than with Deepgram. The model downloads once, on the first call (about 480 MB for Small). A model has to transcribe faster than people talk, so choose a smaller one if the lines fall behind. Pair it with Ollama under API keys for a call that sends nothing off this computer.

                      The libraries for local transcription are not installed in this build, so a call would fail to start.

                      Pick the device your meeting app uses if it is not the Windows default. A device unplugged later falls back to the default.

                      API keys and providers

                      This app uses your own keys: Deepgram to listen, and a model provider to write the lines. Keys are saved only on this computer, in %APPDATA%\Fawa2ly\.env, and this window never shows a saved key again.

                      Listening

                      Not needed if Listening is set to transcribe on this computer.

                      On this computer

                      A model server running here: nothing you say leaves this computer and no key is needed. A server that answers the scan is turned on.

                      Cloud providers

                      Open a provider to add its key, then fetch its models and pick one. Only providers marked Ready appear in the model list.

                      Your own endpoints

                      Any server that speaks the OpenAI chat completions API: a company gateway, LiteLLM, a proxy, or a model server on another computer.
