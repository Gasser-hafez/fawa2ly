Fawa2ly, Daylight, Teleprompter and High contrast. Opacity, text size and click-through on top.

How it works

1

Hears the call

The other side comes from your speakers; your microphone is optional. Each side is its own stream, so it never guesses who spoke.

2

Catches the question

About a second of silence closes a turn. It can ask the model early, while the question is still being finished.

3

Writes your lines

3 to 5 short lines from your CV and your role, streamed onto the overlay as they're written.

4

You say it

Read in order, the lines make one answer. Ask for Shorter, More, a Recap or questions to ask, any time.
